## 📌 Full Form & Concept

- **EDC** stands for **Everyday Carry** — the collection of essential items, tools, devices, and accessories that people carry with them every day to be prepared, productive, and stylish.
- **In EDC Catalyst:** **E**xplore · **D**ecipher · **C**ategory — helping users discover the world's most legendary EDC gear across premier global brands.
